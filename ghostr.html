The Idea

GHOSTR is a concept brand for streetwear and gaming, built around one character: the silent operator. Street style and gaming gear never spoke the same language, so GHOSTR speaks both, with high impact and low profile.

The gap

Two worlds, one audience, and no brand that belonged to both.

The idea

A ghost that moves like lightning, seen only when it chooses to be.

The attitude

The R in GHOSTR stands for Rush, Rage and Rising: the energy of a player.

The Identity

The logo merges a ghost with a lightning bolt. Bold shapes and high contrast keep it clear on screen and easy to print on fabric.

  • Three colors only, easy to print
  • Bold graphics with high contrast
  • Clean and scalable on any product

What This Project Includes

Three parts, from the brand’s core to how people meet it.

Logo, three-color palette and typography system.

Content

Campaign visuals and a moodboard for social media and video.

The Brand In Use

The identity applied to hoodie mockups, a street campaign concept and a visual world that carries the same mood everywhere.
